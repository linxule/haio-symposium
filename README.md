# HAIO Symposium 2024

The [HAIO Symposium archive](https://haiosymposium.com/) brings together the speakers, program and papers from “Human & Artificial Intelligence in Organization: Interdisciplinary Impact.”

The event took place on September 9, 2024, at the I-X Hub, Imperial College London, White City. It explored human–AI cooperation and the choices involved in building trustworthy AI.

## Sessions and papers

The first session examined similarities and differences between human and artificial intelligence. Presentations covered warmth and competence in human–AI cooperation, and language models' performance on theory of mind tasks. Kevin R. McKee and Winnie Street presented, with Martin Anthony as respondent.

The second session examined openness, ethics, reliability and transparency in AI. Presentations covered participatory AI collaboration and governance, and human interaction evaluations of AI harms and risks. Jennifer Ding and Lujain Ibrahim presented, with Aidan Peppin as respondent.

The [session summaries and paper links](https://haiosymposium.com/#session-1) let you explore the research behind both discussions.
